## 3. Full Description (Character Count: 2,722 / 4,000)

```text
Transform cluttered spaces into peaceful sanctuaries in this cozy room sort puzzle. When everyday life gets overwhelming, step into charming, lived-in rooms waiting for a thoughtful touch. Your goal is simple yet deeply rewarding: spot matching everyday items scattered across layered piles, pack them neatly into labeled boxes, and organize room after room into a calm, beautiful haven.

A Satisfying Room Sort Experience
Every level presents a unique themed room—from a sunlit living room with misplaced books and pillows, to a bustling bakery counter, a cozy artisan cafe, or a plant-filled bedroom studio. Look through the cozy clutter to find what each storage box needs. Items without an immediate matching box rest in your temporary waiting slots. Because slots are limited and piles are layered like a classic tile tower, planning which objects to clear first keeps your spaces open and your mind engaged.

Clean, Tidy, and Then Decorate!
Unlike ordinary sorting games, the journey doesn't end when the last box is taped shut. Once a messy room is completely tidied up, you unlock the delight of room restoration. Choose stylish furniture, hang soothing fairy lights, place vibrant house plants, and customize the decor to make each space uniquely yours. Watch each environment transition from scattered chaos into a warm, harmonious retreat you will love revisiting.

Thoughtful, Low-Stress Puzzles
- Layered Clutter Challenges: Objects rest in realistic, natural piles. Carefully pick items from the top layers to uncover the treasures tucked beneath.
- Limited Temporary Slots: Keep an eye on your storage trays. Clear matching groups to free up room for your next move.
- Room Restoration: Earn stars by completing rooms and spend them to decorate, furnish, and restore each aesthetic interior.
- Replay for Mastery: Revisit earlier rooms to improve your clear times and test cleaner packing strategies.
- Mindful Relaxation: Enjoy gentle, tactile organizing whenever you need a peaceful mental reset during your day.

Gentle Pacing & Player-First Design
Relax to soft lo-fi melodies and satisfying, tactile sound effects with every tap and box closure. There are no punishing countdown timers or rush to finish. If you ever find your temporary slots full on a tricky layout, optional rewarded ads are available to grant extra holding slots or helpful hints. You will never lose your level progress if you choose not to use them—your room state is preserved so you can retry with fresh eyes whenever you are ready.

Whether you have five quiet minutes before bed or an afternoon to unwind, experience the simple, soothing joy of turning mess into harmony. Grab a box, clear the floor, and find your calm today.
```
